# Correctness evidence — PR #54706 JartX closure (W7900/gfx1100)

Reference: FP32 dequantized GEMM
    W[k, n] = (q[k, n] - 8) * float(round_to_dtype(scale[g, n])),  g = k // 128
    ref = x_f32 @ W_f32
Synthetic weights: q ~ U[0,16), scale ~ N(0.02, 0.01), x ~ N(0,1) in the
activation dtype. Shape-dependent magnitudes: |ref| ~ N(0, ~7) at K=4096.

## 1. Deterministic path (this PR) — measured max_abs_error

| path  | dtype | shape (M, K, N=4096)   | k_split | max_abs | mean_abs |
|-------|-------|------------------------|---------|---------|----------|

| scalar| bf16  | 1, 6656                | 26      | 0.0625  | 0.0098   |
| scalar| fp16  | 1, 4096                | 16      | 0.9578  | 0.1892   |
| scalar| fp16  | 1, 6656                | 26      | 0.9077  | 0.1945   |
| wmma  | bf16  | 16, 4096 (16x16_1w)    | 4       | 0.0887  | 0.0112   |
| wmma  | bf16  | 64, 4096 (64x64_4w)    | 4       | 0.0968  | 0.0112   |

These are the error floors of the kernels' own rounding structure:

* scalar bf16: FP32 end-to-end; only the final cast rounds. Measured
  0.0625 == 1 ulp_bf16 at the output magnitude (|ref| up to ~20) — exactly
  the round-to-nearest bound.
* wmma bf16: B narrowed to bf16 per cell (one rounding), FP32 accumulate.
  mean_abs 0.0112 at K=4096 scales with sqrt(K) as expected.
* wmma fp16: B narrowed via the precise sub-then-mul variant (~1e-4/cell);
  mean_abs 0.0014.
* scalar fp16: the classic exllama bit-trick rounds the per-(group, col)
  offset constant scale*(-1024-zero) to fp16 (~0.008 at scale 0.02); that
  noise accumulates across groups (sigma ~ 0.24 at K=4096). Pre-existing
  dequant behaviour shared with exllama — NOT epilogue error; unchanged by
  this PR (identical before/after).

bf16 headline (scale 0.02 synthetic weights): max_abs 0.24-0.37 -> 0.06
(4-6x). fp16 WMMA: 0.024-0.038 -> 0.011-0.018 (2x). The old PR reported
"0.028 -> 0.0061" (4.6x) from real Muse weights with a different scale
distribution — same factor, and that number is bf16, not fp16.

## 3. Determinism: A/B control

Legacy build, bit-repeatability over repeated identical calls:
* 12/20 benchmark shapes produced differing outputs (all shapes with
  active split contention: scalar K=4096/6656 at M=1/8/16, WMMA k_split=4
  at M<=128 in at least one dtype).
* Test suite on the legacy build: 8/20 FAILED
  (test_scalar_splitk / scalar_small_m / wmma_splitk / wmma_large_m[fp16]
  repeatability + test_scalar_matches_fp32_reference[bf16] with
  max_abs=0.3022 > tol=0.10), 12 passed (incl. all single-split tests,
  which are deterministic under both epilogues).

Fixed build: 20/20 PASS; bit-repeatable on all 20 benchmark shapes.

## 4. Scratch-coverage empirical check (at::empty safety)

The at::empty FP32 scratch passes all reference-correctness tests on both
paths and both dtypes (any reducer-visible unwritten slot would surface as
allocator garbage / NaN, 6+ orders above tolerance). Before/after closure
numerics are bit-identical on all 20 benchmark shapes — zero-init was
provably dead traffic, consistent with the coverage invariant documented
at alloc_wmma_partials in csrc/rocm/q_gemm_rdna3_wmma.cu.
